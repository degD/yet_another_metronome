# YAM Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

This Privacy Policy explains how Yet Another Metronome (YAM), Android package
`com.degeder.yam`, handles information.

## Summary

YAM does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.
YAM does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or third-party
data collection services.

## Information Stored on Your Device

YAM stores your selected light or dark theme preference in local app storage on
your device. This preference is not sent to the developer or any third party.

You can remove this information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling
the app.

## Device Access

YAM uses audio output to play the metronome sounds and uses platform APIs for
status-bar and safe-area display behavior. YAM does not access your contacts,
location, camera, microphone, files, or device identifiers.

## Network Access

The Android app does not transmit personal information or usage data over the
network. Links to the source repository and license are optional links that may
open in your browser. Websites opened through those links have their own
privacy policies.

## Google Play

If you obtain YAM through Google Play, Google may process purchase, account,
and device information under Google's own privacy policy. YAM does not receive
your payment details and does not collect personal information from Google
Play.

## Children's Privacy

YAM does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The current
version will be published at:

`https://degd.github.io/privacypolicies/yam/`
